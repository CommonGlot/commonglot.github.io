What is CommonGlot?

The CommonGlot Foundation supports GlotSuite, a comprehensive collection of open-source tools, models, corpora, and benchmarks designed to democratize natural language processing for the world's languages.

While most NLP research focuses on a handful of high-resource languages, thousands of languages remain underserved. Our projects address this gap by providing language identification for 2,000+ languages, curating web-scale corpora for 1,000+ languages, building multilingual models for 500+ languages, and benchmarking OCR across 158 writing systems.

All our work is developed at CIS-LMU Munich and Sorbonne Université, and published at top venues including ACL, EMNLP, NeurIPS, and WWW. Every tool, dataset, and model is freely available under open licenses.
