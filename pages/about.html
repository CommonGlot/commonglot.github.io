Our Mission

The CommonGlot Foundation exists to ensure that advances in natural language processing benefit every language community, not just the few hundred languages that dominate the digital world.

We build and maintain GlotSuite — a growing collection of open-source tools, models, corpora, and benchmarks that together provide the infrastructure needed to bring NLP technology to thousands of underserved languages. From language identification and script detection to web-scale corpus curation and multilingual modeling, our tools form a complete pipeline for multilingual research.

All of our work is freely available under open licenses. We believe that language technology should be a public good, accessible to researchers, educators, and communities everywhere.

How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from researchers, engineers, linguists, and language community members. Here are some ways to get involved:

  • Report issues — Found a bug or misclassification? Open an issue on the relevant GitHub repository.
  • Contribute data — Help expand coverage by contributing text data for underrepresented languages.
  • Improve models — Submit pull requests to improve language identification, script detection, or other tools.
  • Evaluate and test — Help benchmark our tools on your language and report results.
  • Spread the word — Share our tools with researchers and communities who could benefit.
  • Cite our work — If you use GlotSuite tools in your research, please cite the relevant papers.
