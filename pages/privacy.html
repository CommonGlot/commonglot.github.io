Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

CommonGlot is a non-profit initiative. We are committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to our website. This policy describes what information we collect and how we use it.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and referring page as part of their standard hosting service. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details on their data practices.

Information We Collect

We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics services on this website. We do not collect personal information from visitors.

Third-Party Services

This site loads resources from the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — to display typefaces for different writing systems. Google may log requests; see Google Fonts privacy information.
  • jsDelivr CDN — to serve additional font files. See jsDelivr's privacy policy.
  • CARTO — to display map tiles on the Languages page. See CARTO's privacy policy.

External Links

This site links to external services including GitHub, Hugging Face, arXiv, and the ACL Anthology. These services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage solely to remember your light/dark theme preference. This data stays in your browser and is never transmitted to any server.

Data and Code

All datasets, models, and code referenced on this site are released under their respective open-source licenses. Please refer to each project's repository for specific licensing terms.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact us via our GitHub organization.
